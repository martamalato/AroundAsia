Kenting

Located at the southern tip of Taiwan, Kenting is the island's premier beach destination. Known for its tropical climate, turquoise waters, coral reefs, dramatic coastal scenery and laid-back atmosphere, it feels very different from Taiwan's bustling cities. For many Taiwanese, Kenting is the ultimate weekend getaway.

History

The Kenting region has been inhabited for thousands of years by indigenous peoples, including the Paiwan community. Due to its strategic location at the meeting point of the Taiwan Strait and the Pacific Ocean, the area has long been important for fishing and maritime trade.

In 1984, Kenting became Taiwan's first national park. Today, Kenting National Park protects a unique combination of tropical forests, coral reefs, grasslands and coastal ecosystems.

Nature & Beaches

Kenting is famous for having some of Taiwan's best beaches. The warm tropical climate allows beach activities almost year-round, while the surrounding waters are popular for surfing, snorkeling, diving and paddleboarding.

  • More than 300 sunny days per year.
  • Tropical temperatures throughout most of the year.
  • Coral reefs surround large parts of the coastline.
  • One of the best places in Taiwan for water sports.
  • Unique landscapes where mountains meet the ocean.

Fun Facts

  • Kenting National Park was Taiwan's first national park.
  • The region has a tropical climate, unlike most of Taiwan.
  • The southernmost point of Taiwan is located near Kenting.
  • The area is home to monkeys, deer, and many tropical bird species.
  • Strong ocean currents from different seas meet near Kenting.
  • Kenting hosts one of Taiwan's most famous annual music festivals.
  • The coral reefs around Kenting support hundreds of marine species.
  • Most beautiful beaches cannot actually be visited. Areas such as Shadao Beach are strictly protected to preserve the coral ecosystem.
  • The region is popular with both local tourists and international surfers.

Our Impression

Kenting felt completely different from the rest of Taiwan. After spending time in cities like Taipei, arriving here felt like stepping into a tropical beach destination where life moves at a slower pace.

What we enjoyed most was the combination of beautiful coastline, warm weather and easygoing atmosphere. Renting a scooter and driving between beaches, viewpoints and coastal roads was one of the highlights of our stay. Watching the ocean from Longpan Park and ending the day at the night market made Kenting a perfect place to relax before continuing our journey.
