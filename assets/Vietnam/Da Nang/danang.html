Da Nang

Da Nang is Vietnam's modern coastal city, located halfway between Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. Known for its long sandy beaches, relaxed atmosphere and rapid development, it combines city life, mountain scenery and easy access to some of Central Vietnam's most famous attractions.

History

For centuries, Da Nang served as an important trading port for the ancient Champa Kingdom. Its strategic location on the central coast made it a gateway for merchants traveling between Asia and Europe.

During French colonial rule, the city was known as Tourane and became an important administrative and military center. In more recent decades, Da Nang transformed into one of Vietnam's fastest-growing cities, with major investments in infrastructure, tourism and technology.

Fun Facts

  • Da Nang is often considered one of Vietnam's cleanest cities.
  • The Dragon Bridge breathes fire and water on weekend evenings.
  • My Khe Beach was once listed among the world's most beautiful beaches.
  • The city sits between the mountains and the South China Sea.
  • Da Nang is a popular base for visiting Hoi An and Hue.

Our Impression

Da Nang felt calmer and more spacious than Vietnam's larger cities. The combination of beaches, modern cafés, mountain viewpoints and great food made it an easy place to slow down for a few days. Watching the Dragon Bridge light up at night and exploring the nearby Marble Mountains were some of the highlights of our stay.
